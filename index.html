The Problem

AI AGENTS CREATE A NEW ATTACK SURFACE

Traditional security tools were built for web apps and APIs. They don't understand AI agents. They can't detect prompt injection. They can't spot a jailbreak. They have no idea what "tool misuse" even means.

Meanwhile, every company is rushing to deploy AI agents — customer support bots, coding assistants, data analysts — without any security layer protecting them.

One successful attack can leak user data, compromise your entire system, or turn your AI agent against your own users. The question isn't if you'll be attacked. It's whether you'll know when it happens.
